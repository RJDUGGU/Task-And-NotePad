## 1. Overview
This Privacy Policy describes how the **Task & Notepad** mobile application ("we", "our", or "us") manages and protects your information when you use our Android application.

Task & Notepad is designed as an offline-first productivity tool for managing daily tasks, to-do lists, and personal notes. We place the highest priority on user privacy and data security.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
**We do NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal information.**

- **User Content (Tasks & Notes):** All task titles, descriptions, categories, priority tags, due dates, and note contents created within the app are stored exclusively in your device's local internal database (Android Room SQLite DB). This information never leaves your device.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, log in, or provide personal details (name, email address, phone number, etc.) to use this application.
- **No Device Identifiers or Telemetry:** We do not collect advertising IDs, IMEI numbers, IP addresses, or device location data.

---

## 3. Permissions Used
Task & Notepad does not request or require sensitive runtime permissions. It does not access:
- Camera or microphone
- Contacts or phone call logs
- SMS or messaging records
- Precise or coarse location
- External shared media or storage

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & Advertisements
- **Zero Third-Party Trackers:** We do not embed third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or social trackers.
- **No Ads:** The application is ad-free and does not communicate with ad exchanges or marketing brokers.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion
Because all data resides strictly on your local device:
- You have complete control over your content.
- Deleting a task or note removes it immediately from local storage.
- Clearing the app data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored tasks and notes from your device.

---

## 6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
Task & Notepad is suitable for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children or any other users.
